Fractals are shapes or patterns that 
repeat themselves at different scales. 
This means that if you zoom in on a small 
part of a fractal, it looks similar to the 
whole! Fractals are found throughout 
nature — like in the branching of trees, 
in vegetables, such as cauliflower or 
broccoli, in the shape of snowflakes, or 
in the patterns of coastlines. They can be 
created using simple rules but can form 
very complex and beautiful designs. 
Fractals help us understand patterns 
in nature, and also lead us to important 
concepts in mathematics.
