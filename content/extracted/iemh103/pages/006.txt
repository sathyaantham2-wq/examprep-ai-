Think and Reflect
Why does a negative times a negative equal a positive? Think of it in 
terms of action and debt. If a negative number represents a debt, then 
multiplying by a negative number represents the removal of that debt. 
(Hint: If someone takes away (–) four of your debts that are each 
worth `3 (that is, –3), you are effectively `12 richer! Therefore, 
(–3) × (– 4) = +12.)
