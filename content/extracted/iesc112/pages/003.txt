India’s Scientific Contributions
Some ancient Indian traditions, such as the Sangam Tinai classification of 
landscapes and the protection of sacred groves, demonstrate a sophisticated 
literary and cultural understanding of landscapes, and their biota. These 
customs effectively preserved locally diverse habitats, aligning with 
contemporary ecological principles, even if it is not articulated as a formal 
ecological theory.
