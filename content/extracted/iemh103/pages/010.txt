3.4.1 Representation of Rational Numbers on the 
Number Line
We already know that integers can be represented on a number line. 
To do this, we first choose a point and mark it as 0, called the origin. 
Moving one unit to the right gives the point representing 1, two units to 
the right gives 2, and so on. Similarly, moving one unit to the left of the 
origin gives –1, two units to the left gives –2, and so on. Each integer lies 
at an equal distance from the next one. This is represented in Fig. 3.3.
