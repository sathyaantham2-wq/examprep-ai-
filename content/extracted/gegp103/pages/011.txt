What will be the sum of 15 3/10 4/100 and 2 6/10 8/100?
This can be solved in different ways. Some are shown below.
(a) Method 1
(15 + 2) + (3/10 + 6/10) + (4/100 + 8/100)
= 17 + 9/10 + 12/100
= 17 + 9/10 + 1/10 + 2/100
= 17 + 10/10 + 2/100
= 18 2/100.
(b) Method 2 (column form): 15 3/10 4/100 + 2 6/10 8/100 = 17 9/10 12/100
= 17 10/10 2/100 = 18 2/100
(10 hundredths is the same as 1 tenth.)
