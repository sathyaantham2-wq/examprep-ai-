Can we extend this further?
What will the fraction be when 1/100 is split into 10 equal parts?
It will be 1/1000, i.e., a thousand such parts make up a unit.

Just as when we extend to the left of 10,000, we get bigger place
values at each step, we can also extend to the right of 1/1000, getting
smaller place values at each step.
(Place value chain: 10,000 <-> 1000 <-> 100 <-> 10 <-> 1 <-> 1/10 <-> 1/100,
each step x10 / /10; and a zoomed number-line diagram from 0 to 1 showing
tenths, then 60/100 to 70/100 zoomed to hundredths, then 660/1000 to
670/1000 zoomed to thousandths.)
