Is the black-and-white region in Fig. 6.14 tileable?
Any region tiled with black-and-white-tiles must have an equal number 
of black tiles and white tiles. 
Since the black-and-white region in Fig. 6.14 has 8 white squares and 6 
black squares, it can never be tiled with these tiles!
Use this idea to find another unit square that, when removed from 
a 5 × 3 grid, makes it non-tileable? 
Isn’t it surprising how, by introducing colours and making the 
problem more complicated, it becomes easier to tackle? What a creative 
way of looking at the problem!
