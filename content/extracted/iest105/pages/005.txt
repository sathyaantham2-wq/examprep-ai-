 Samhitās primarily contain hymns used 
for invoking the deity and for offering 
oblations in the yajña.
 The Brāhmaṇa, composed in 
prose, offer explanations for ritual 
performances.
 The Āraṇyakas (araṇya = forest) expand 
on philosophical speculations of sages 
living in forests.

the Self (Ātman) and the Ultimate
