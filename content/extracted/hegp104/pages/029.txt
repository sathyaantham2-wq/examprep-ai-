Properties of a rhombus —
y The opposite sides of a rhombus are parallel to each other.
y In a rhombus, the adjacent angles add up to 180°, and the 
opposite angles are equal.
y The diagonals of a rhombus bisect each other at right 
angles.
y The diagonals of a rhombus bisect its angles.
 A kite is a quadrilateral with two non-overlapping adjacent 
pairs of sides having the same length.
 A trapezium is a quadrilateral having at least one pair of 
parallel opposite sides.
 The sum of the angle measures in a quadrilateral is 360°.
