 is

Solution Since R1
and R2

, and (a, a) ∈ R2 ∀ a ∈A.
This implies that (a, a) ∈ R1 ∩ R2
, ∀ a, showing R1 ∩ R2
 is reflexive. Further,
(a, b) ∈ R1 ∩ R2 ⇒ (a, b) ∈ R1
 and (a, b) ∈ R2 ⇒ (b, a) ∈ R1
 and (b, a) ∈ R2 ⇒
(b, a) ∈ R1 ∩ R2
, hence, R1 ∩ R2
is symmetric. Similarly, (a, b) ∈ R1 ∩ R2
and
(b, c) ∈ R1 ∩ R2 ⇒ (a, c) ∈ R1

. This shows that
R1 ∩ R2
is transitive. Thus, R1 ∩ R2
is an equivalence relation.
