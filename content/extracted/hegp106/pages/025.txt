3. Find 3 examples where the product of two numbers remains 
unchanged when one of them is increased by 2 and the other is 
decreased by 4. 
Ans. Let numbers be a and b. 
Then (a + 2) (b – 4) = ab
So, b = 2a + 4
Hence 3 examples are (a = 1, b = 6), (a = 2, b = 8), (a = 3, b = 10)
