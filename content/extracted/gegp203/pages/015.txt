Finding Common Ground
This is similar to the procedure for prime factorisation. At each step, 
the two numbers are divided by a common prime factor, and the two 
quotients are written down in the next row. This continues till we get 
two numbers that do not have any common prime factors.
How do we use this to find the HCF of 84 and 180? Explore.
