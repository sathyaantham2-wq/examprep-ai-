How do we know what we know about China? 
China has an abundance of historical records 
as official historiographers recorded events. 
However, the earliest source of information 
about China are the ‘oracles’, which were 
symbols made on pieces of animal bones 
and tortoise shells. These bones were 
heated until they cracked, and 
interpretations were made based on the 
patterns of the cracks. These oracles were 
often used to foretell the future, and today, 
they tell us a lot about the hopes, desires, 
and fears of the early Chinese.
