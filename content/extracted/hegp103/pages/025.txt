Note that when a number is grouped into powers of 60 for its 
representation, no power of 60 can occur 60 or more times. If this 
happens, then 60 of them can be grouped to form the next power of 60. 
For example, consider the expression—
(1) × 3600 + (70) × 60 + 2 = (1) × 602
 + (60 + 10) × 60 + 2
= (1) × 602
 + 602 + (10) × 60 + 2
= (2) × 602 + (10) × 60 + 2
Therefore, any number can be represented using the numerals from 
1–59, along with the numerals for landmark numbers.
