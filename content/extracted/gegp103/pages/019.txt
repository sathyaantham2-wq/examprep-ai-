3.5 Units of Measurement
Length Conversion
We have been using a scale to measure length for a few years. We
already know that 1 cm = 10 mm (millimeters).
How many cm is 1 mm?
1 mm = 1/10 cm = 0.1 cm (i.e., one-tenth of a cm).
How many cm is (a) 5 mm? (b) 12 mm?
5 mm = 5/10 cm = 0.5 cm
12 mm = 10 mm + 2 mm = 1 cm + 2/10 cm = 1.2 cm.
