Example 7: A farmer harvested 260 kg of wheat last year. This year, they 
harvested 650 kg of wheat. What percentage of last year’s harvest is this 
year’s harvest?
This year’s harvest = 650
260 × 100 = 250% of last year’s harvest.
250% indicates that it is 2.5 times the original value.
