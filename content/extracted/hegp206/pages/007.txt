Calendar Magic
A page from a calendar is given below. Your friend picks a 2 × 2 grid from 
this calendar, adds the 4 numbers in this grid and tells you the sum.
AUGUST 2025
SUN MON TUE WED THU FRI SAT
1 2

24 25 26 27 28 29 30
31
6 7
13 14
6 + 7 + 13 + 14 = 40
Can we find the 4 numbers in the grid from just knowing this sum?
Let us use algebra. Consider a 2 × 2 grid. Let a represent the top left 
number. What are the other numbers in terms of a?
a a + 1
a + 7 a + 8
Adding all four numbers, the sum is a + (a + 1) + (a + 7) + (a + 8) = 4a + 16.
Suppose you are told that the sum is 36. Can you find the 4 numbers in 
the grid?
• 4a + 16 = 36
• 4a = 20 (subtracting 16 from both sides)
• a = 5 (dividing both sides by 4)
Now that we have found a, we know that the other three numbers are 
a + 1, a + 7 and a + 8. Therefore, the grid must be the following:
5 6
12 13
