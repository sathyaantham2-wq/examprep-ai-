Look at the following numbers: 3 6 10 15 1
They are arranged such that each pair of adjacent numbers adds up to 
a square. 
3 + 6 = 9, 6 + 10 = 16, 10 + 15 = 25, 15 + 1 = 16.
Try arranging the numbers 1 to 17 (without repetition) in a row in a 
similar way — the sum of every adjacent pair of numbers should be a 
square. 
Can you arrange them in more than one way? If not, can you explain 
why?
Can you do the same with numbers from 1 to 32 (again, without 
repetition), but this time arranging all the numbers in a circle?
Square Pairs!
