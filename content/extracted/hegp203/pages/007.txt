How do we mark the different slices of the pie chart?
To mark a slice in the pie chart, the angle corresponding to a grade 
should be proportional to the number of students who have scored that 
grade. The total angle in a circle is 360°. So, we need to divide 360 in the 
ratio of 12 : 10 : 8 : 6 : 4.
Can we reduce this ratio to its simplest form?
We can use the same procedure we used to reduce a ratio with two 
terms to its simplest form. We divide all the terms by their HCF to get the 
simplest form.
In this example, 2 is the HCF of the terms. We get the simplest form by 
dividing all the terms by 2. The ratio becomes 6 : 5 : 4 : 3 : 2.
So, the angles are:
Grade A = 6
(6 + 5 + 4 + 3 + 2) × 360° = 6
20 × 360° = 6 × 18 = 108°.
Grade B = 5
(6 + 5 + 4 + 3 + 2) × 360° = 5 × 18 = 90°.
Grade C = 4 × 18 = 72°.
Grade D = 3 × 18 = 54°.
Grade E = 2 × 18 = 36°.
