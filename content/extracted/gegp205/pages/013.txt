Mean and Median are called measures of central tendency, i.e., the 
tendency of the values to pile up around a particular value. In other 
words, they represent the ‘centre’ of the data. 
Of Ends and the Essence
As we have just seen, the mean and the median can give different 
perspectives on the data. As part of analysing data, it can also be valuable 
to look at the variability in the given data, i.e., its extremes (minimum 
and maximum values).
