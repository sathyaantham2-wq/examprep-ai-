4.3 Kinematic Equations for Motion in a Straight 
Line with Constant Acceleration
Let us consider the special case of motion with constant acceleration 
and try to derive some equations for analysing such motions. Since the 
acceleration is constant, acceleration at each instant will be equal to the 
average acceleration over any time interval.
