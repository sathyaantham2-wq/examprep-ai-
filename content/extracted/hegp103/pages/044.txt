1. Why do you think the Chinese alternated between the Zong and Heng
symbols? If only the Zong symbols were to be used, how would 41 be 
represented? Could this numeral be interpreted in any other way if 
there is no significant space between two successive positions? 
Ans. The Chinese alternated between the Zong (vertical) and Hong 
(Horizontal) rod numerals to get all the powers of 10 required to 
get all numbers, and to distinguish between adjacent places.
