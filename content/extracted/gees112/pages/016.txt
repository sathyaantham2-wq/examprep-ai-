Æ Apart from textiles, Surat is home to the largest diamond 
industry in the world. Approximately 1.5 million artisans 
are involved in activities like cutting and polishing 
diamonds on a gigantic scale. Trade flourished here from 
the 16th century onwards. The city’s location on the west 
coast led to the setting up of ports and road networks that 
continue to be important today. Communities of expert 
artisans and skilled persons have lived here for centuries; 
their skills have been passed on over generations making 
it a flourishing city.
