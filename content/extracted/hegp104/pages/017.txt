Let us list the properties of a parallelogram.
Property 1: The opposite sides of a parallelogram are equal. 
Property 2: The opposite sides of a parallelogram are parallel. 
Property 3: In a parallelogram, the adjacent angles add up to 180°, and 
the opposite angles are equal.
Are the diagonals of a parallelogram always equal? Check with the 
parallelogram that you have constructed.
