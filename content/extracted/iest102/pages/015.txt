2 – Shaping of the 
Earth’s Surface
Glaciers
Glacial erosion occurs when glaciers slowly move over the land, 
carving and shaping the landscape. Common landforms made from 
glacial erosion include—U-shaped valleys (formed as glaciers 
widen and deepen river valleys), cirques (bowl-shaped depressions at 
the head of a glacier), aretes (sharp ridges between valleys), hanging 
valleys (which occur where smaller glaciers meet larger ones), and 
fjords (deep, narrow inlets created when the sea floods glacial 
valleys). These landforms are important for humans in several ways.

trekking, skiing, and mountaineering. Fjords are used for harbours
