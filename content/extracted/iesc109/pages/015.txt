(iv) The chemical formula gives the simplest ratio of the elements in a 
compound. Therefore, after criss-crossing, the subscripts are divided 
by a common factor, if any. For example, if we get the subscripts 2 and 
4, they are divided by 2 to get 1 and 2, which are then used as subscripts 
in the formula.
