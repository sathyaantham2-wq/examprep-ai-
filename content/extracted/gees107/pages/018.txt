Fig. 7.15.1 to 7.15.3. In the 3rd image, these terracotta sculptures from the Gupta period 
(Ahichchhatra, western Uttar Pradesh) depict India’s sacred rivers, Ganga and Yamuna. Their 
vāhanas distinguish them: Ganga stands on a makara (a mythical creature akin to a crocodile), 
while Yamuna stands on a tortoise. Water flows over their heads, and the pot is another reminder 
of their manifestation as rivers.
