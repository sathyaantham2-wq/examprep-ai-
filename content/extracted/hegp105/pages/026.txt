4. Find a few numbers that leave a remainder of 2 when divided 
by 3 and a remainder of 2 when divided by 4. Write an algebraic 
expression to describe all such numbers.
Ans. Let 3a + 2 = x and 4b + 2 = x
So, 3a = x – 2 and 4b = x – 2
x – 2 is multiple of 3 and 4.
Take LCM of 3 and 4, which is 12 
The, first number is x – 2 = 12 or x = 14 = 12 × 1 + 2
Other such numbers are 12 × 2 + 2 =26, 12 × 3 + 2 = 38, … or in 
general 12n + 2.
