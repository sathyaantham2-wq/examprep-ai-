4.5 Factorisation Using Algebra Tiles
Consider a rectangle with sides x + 3 and x + 4 units.
We know that the area of such a rectangle is (x + 3) (x + 4) sq. units.
Using distributivity, we get 
(x + 3)(x + 4) = x2
 + 3x + 4x + 12 = x2
 + 7x + 12.
