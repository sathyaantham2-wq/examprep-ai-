Water: Rain-Fed vs. Irrigated Agriculture
Water is a fundamental resource for agriculture. Without it, 
crops cannot grow. The availability of water is a significant 
factor that determines how farming is done in different parts 
of India. This section examines two primary types of irrigation 
practices: rain-fed and irrigated.
a. Rain-fed agriculture
