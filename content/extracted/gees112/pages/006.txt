Prices and Markets
What happens when there are many buyers and sellers in the 
market? How do prices respond to how buyers and sellers 
interact in the market? 
In the figure given below, the seller wants to sell the guavas at 
₹80 per kg. However, buyers may not be willing to buy at that 
price. If a buyer finds the price very high, he will ask for a price 
that he is willing to pay. The seller may not be willing to sell at the 
price offered by the buyer because it would not be profitable for 
him. The buyer and seller negotiate until a mutually agreeable 
price is reached. The transaction can then be completed. If such 
a point is not reached, i.e. the point where the buyer and the 
seller agree on a price, the transaction may not take place.
