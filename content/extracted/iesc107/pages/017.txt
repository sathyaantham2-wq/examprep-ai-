An inclined plane is a simple machine that helps move a heavy load 
to a higher (or lower) level. Let us now find its mechanical advantage. 
Let the mass of the object be m. Then, the load is the weight mg of the 
object to be lifted. Let F  ′ be the force required to raise the object to a height 
h (Fig. 7.28a). Then, F  ′ is the effort. Let the length of the inclined plane be 
L (Fig. 7.28b).
