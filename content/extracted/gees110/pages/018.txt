Before we move on …
Æ The Constitution of India is a guiding book or ‘rulebook’ which 
protects the rights of all citizens. Citizens are also expected to 
follow the fundamental duties of the Constitution. 
Æ The rich civilizational heritage of India, the freedom struggle 
and good practices from the constitutions of other countries 
served as the building blocks of the Indian Constitution.
Æ Its key features define the social, economic, and political 
structure of the country and its parliamentary form of 
government.
Æ It is a living document that can be improved as per the country’s 
needs.
