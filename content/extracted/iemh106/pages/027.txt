Why were human beings so fond of using circular shapes? Was this only 
for practical reasons, or could there have been other reasons too? What 
kinds of uses have human beings found for the circular shape?
Early on, human beings faced the challenge of finding the area A 
enclosed by a circle. They may have needed, for example, to work out 
how much grain can be kept in a cylindrical tower (which has a circular 
cross section), or how much area is occupied by a circular garden or a 
circular building. They needed such data for planning out their cities, 
for tax purposes, etc.
Early societies understood very well that the area A must be
