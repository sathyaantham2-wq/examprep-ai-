Property Involving both the HCF and the LCM
Which is greater — the LCM of two numbers or their product?
You could analyse the above statement using examples. Then try to reason 
or prove, why the LCM is never greater than the product of the numbers. 
[Hint: Is the product also a common multiple of the two numbers?]
There is an interesting relation between the product of two numbers 
and their HCF and LCM.
Consider the numbers 105 and 95. Find their LCM.
Factorising them into their primes:
105 = 3 × 5 × 7
95 = 5 × 19
LCM = 3 × 5 × 7 × 19.
Let us consider the product in the factorised form: 
105 × 95 = 3 × 5 × 5 × 7 × 19
Is the LCM a factor of the product? If yes, what should it be multiplied 
with to get the product? It can be seen that
105 × 95 = LCM × 5.
