I’m Up and Down, and 
Round and Round
Humanity has always been fascinated by the shapes of the things 
around them. In some early cave paintings, the sun is depicted as a 
circle. In the cave paintings of Gudahandi in Odisha, one sees numerous 
geometric patterns including triangles, squares, circles, and ovals. 
These shapes were likely inspired by what humans saw in nature.
