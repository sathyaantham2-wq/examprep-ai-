11India and the World: Land and the People 1 – The Story of Indian Farming
Soils of India
India has a diverse range of soil types, each with its distinct 
properties. Ancient texts, such as the Amarakoṣha, describe 
twelve types of agricultural land based on soil fertility. Today, we 
classify the soils of India into six major types: alluvial soil, black 
soil, red soil, laterite soil, desert soil, and mountain / alpine soil.

Soil-crop linkages
In addition to the agroclimatic conditions that we briefly saw 
above, crops are also strongly influenced by the type of soil 
where they grow.
