Think and Reflect
Since ΔABD and ΔACD have equal area, you may wonder — Can we 
divide ΔABD using straight cuts into two or more pieces that we can then 
rearrange to exactly cover ΔACD? What do you think? Is it possible?
We shall spoil the surprise by revealing that it is possible. But we 
will not tell you the least number of pieces required. Try to find the 
answer!
