Figure it Out
1. How many different magic squares can be made using the
numbers 1 – 9? 
2. Create a magic square using the numbers 2 – 10. What strategy 
would you use for this? Compare it with the magic squares 
made using 1 – 9.
3. Take a magic square, and 
(a) increase each number by 1
(b) double each number
In each case, is the resulting grid also a magic square? How do the 
magic sums change in each case?
4. What other operations can be performed on a magic square to 
yield another magic square?
5. Discuss ways of creating a magic square using any set of 9 
consecutive numbers (like 2 – 10, 3 – 11, 9 – 17, etc.).
