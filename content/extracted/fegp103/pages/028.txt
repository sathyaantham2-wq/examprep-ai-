Q. Can we make 1,000 using the numbers in the middle? Why not? What about 14,000,
15,000 and 16,000? Yes, it is possible. Explore how. What thousands cannot be made?
Ans. No; the only number which is smaller than 1000 is 400 and 1000 is not a multiple of 400. 
14000 = 1500 × 8 + 400 × 5
= 12000 + 2000
= 14000
15000 = 13000 + 400 × 5
= 13000 + 2000
= 15000
16000 = 1500 × 8 + 400 × 10
= 12000 + 4000
= 16000
Only one thousand (1000) cannot be made.
