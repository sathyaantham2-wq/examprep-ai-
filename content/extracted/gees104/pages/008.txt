The Varṇa–Jāti System
We saw earlier how human societies grew more complex 
with the rise of civilisation. Whenever this happens, a 
society organises itself in several groups based on class, 
occupation or some other criteria. For instance, there 
could be different groups concerned with governance, 
administration, religion, education, trade, town-planning, 
farming, crafts, arts and all kinds of other professions.
