Patterns in Integer Multiplication
We have explored the multiplication of integers in cases where the 
multiplier is positive, when it is negative, when the multiplicand 
is positive and when it is negative. Using this understanding, let us 
construct a sequence of multiplications and observe the patterns.
What do you notice in this pattern? Can you 
describe it?
We can see that, when the multiplicand is 
positive, for every unit decrease in the multiplier 
the product decreases by the multiplicand.
