Division of Integers
We have earlier seen how division can be converted into multiplication. 
For example, (– 100) ÷ 25 can be reframed as, ‘what should be multiplied
to 25 to get (– 100)?’. That is,
25 × ? = (– 100).
We know that 
25 × (– 4) = (– 100).
Therefore,
(– 100) ÷ 25 = (– 4).
Similarly, (– 100) ÷ (– 4) can be reframed as, ‘What should be multiplied 
to (– 4) to get (– 100)?’
(– 4) × ? = (– 100).
We know that
(– 4) × 25 = (– 100).
Therefore,
(– 100) ÷ (– 4) = 25.
Similarly, we know that
(– 25) × (– 2) = 50.
Therefore,
50 ÷ (– 25) = (– 2).
