1 – The Story of Indian Farming
 DON’T MISS OUT
Before the Arabic terms for the three types of crops became 
more widely used, they were called kedāra (wet crops), 
haimana (winter crops), and graiṣhmika (summer crops).
Each of these crop seasons presents its own unique challenges 
as well. For example, the kharif crops are dependent on the 
timely arrival of the monsoons. Excessive rainfall also leads to 
the destruction of crops, causing significant distress to farmers 
and their families in particular.
