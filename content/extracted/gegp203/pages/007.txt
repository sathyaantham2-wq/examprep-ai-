Example 3: Find the common factors and the HCF of 96 and 275.
We have
96 = 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 2 × 3
275 = 5 × 5 × 11.
There is no subpart that is common amongst these two factorisations. 
So, 1 is the only common factor. It is also their HCF.
