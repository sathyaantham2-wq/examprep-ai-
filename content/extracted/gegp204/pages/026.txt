Ganita Prakash | Grade 7 | Part-II
In 1000 calendar years, how many years are divisible by 400? 2.
In 1000 calendar years, how many years are divisible by 100 but not 
divisible by 400? 10 – 2 = 8.
In 1000 calendar years, how many years are divisible by 4 but not 
divisible by 100 and 400? 250 – 10 = 240.
The rest of the years are 1000 – (2 + 8 + 240) = 750.
So, the total number of days in 1000 calendar years is
(750 × 365) + (240 × 366) + (8 × 365) + (2 × 366) = 3,65,242 days.
The Earth needs 3,65,242.2 days to go around the Sun 1000 times. 
Hence, in 1000 years, the calendar year is slightly shorter (by 0.2 
days) than the actual number of days the Earth takes to go around 
the Sun. 
The calendar makers did not want to bother about a small difference 
that would happen in 1000 years! So, they left the scheme of leap years 
as is …
