LET’S EXPLORE 
You have studied about the Harappan script in your previous 
grades. Observe this seal carefully and discuss why the script has 
not yet been deciphered. Also, find out what efforts have been 
made to decipher it. You can use your textbook, consult your 
teacher, or refer to online sources.
From about 400 BCE, a script known as Brahmi was used in parts 
of southern India and in the Ganga Valley (northern India). It was 
later formalised by the Emperor Aśhoka (3rd century BCE) and 
contemporary Iron Age people all over southern India. You will learn 
more about the journey of scripts in higher grades.
