Suppose Kishanlal achieved a sales of ₹80,000 last month. Out of this, 
the amount he spent on buying these goods he sold last month was 
₹48,000. The difference amount, ₹80,000 – ₹48,000 = ₹32,000, is called 
gross profit.
After deducting the other expenses he incurred (such as 
transport cost, employee’s salary, electricity bill, etc.) amounting 
to ₹8,000, the amount remaining is called net profit, which is 
₹32,000 – ₹8,000 = ₹24,000. 
Note: In this chapter, the term profit refers to gross profit.
