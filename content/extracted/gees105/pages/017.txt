The story of Kauṭilya
According to Buddhist texts, Kauṭilya—sometimes referred to as 
Chāṇakya or Viṣhnugupta—was a teacher at the world-renowned 
Takṣhaśhila (modern-day Taxila) university. His legendary tale 
begins in the court of Dhana Nanda, who as we saw, had become 
highly unpopular.
