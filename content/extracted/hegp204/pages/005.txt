Fractals in Art
Fractals have also long been used in human-made art! Perhaps the 
oldest such fractals appear in the temples of India. An example occurs 
in the Kandariya Mahadev Temple in Khajuraho, Madhya Pradesh 
which was completed in around 1025 C.E.; there one sees a tall temple 
structure, which is made up of smaller copies of the full structure, 
on which there are even smaller copies of the same structure, and 
so on. Fractal-like patterns also occur in temples in Madurai, Hampi, 
Rameswaram, Varanasi, among many others.
Kandariya Mahadev Temple
Fractals are also common in traditional African cultures. For example, 
patterns on Nigerian Fulani wedding blankets often exhibit fractal 
structures.
