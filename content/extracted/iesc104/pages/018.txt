When brakes are applied to a moving vehicle, it moves some distance before 
coming to a stop. The distance travelled depends upon the velocity of the vehicle 
when the brakes are applied, the road surface (wet/dry, etc.), the braking capacity 
of the vehicle (the negative acceleration caused by the brakes) as well as the 
driver’s reaction time. Can you now understand why it is important to maintain 
a safe distance from the vehicle moving ahead of your vehicle (Fig. 4.20) and
