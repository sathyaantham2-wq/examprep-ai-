Fig. 6.17: Area of a parallelogram. Here, A'B'C'D' is a copy of ABCD.
Fig. 6.17 shows how to transform a parallelogram ABCD into a 
rectangle EB'C'F (at right; here, parallelogram A'B'C'D' is a copy of 
ABCD) with the same base b and same height h. Though the two shapes 
are different, their areas are the same. So, the area of the parallelogram 
is equal to base × height = bh.
