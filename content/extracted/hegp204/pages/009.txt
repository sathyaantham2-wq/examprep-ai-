While discussing solids that contain plane surfaces in their boundaries, 
the notion of faces, edges and vertices serves a useful purpose.
Vertex Edge

Vertex Edge
Face
Faces are the plane/flat surfaces of a solid that form its boundary. 
Edges are the line segments that form the sides of the faces, and vertices
are the points at which the edges meet. For example, a cuboid or cube 
has 6 faces, 12 edges and 8 vertices.
