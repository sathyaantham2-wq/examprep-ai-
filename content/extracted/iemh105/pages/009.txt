Theorem 3: Chords of a circle that subtend equal angles at the centre 
are equal.
Let us draw the figure, as in Fig. 5.11.

Fig. 5.11
Given: ∠ACB = ∠DCE. 
To show: AB = ED. 
Why is this true? We see that 
AC = BC = the radius of the circle. 
Likewise, EC = DC = the radius. 
So, AC = DC and BC = EC. 
Since, ∠ACB = ∠DCE, by the SAS 
congruence, ∆ACB ≅ ∆DCE. Hence, 
AB = ED.
