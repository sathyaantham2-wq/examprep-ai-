The hills of the Northeast
Stay on the map as we move towards the hills of the Northeast, 
our final destination for now. Can you see Garo, Khasi, and 
Jantia marked on the map? These hills, part of the Meghalaya 
Plateau, are known for their lush greenery, heavy rainfall, and 
breathtaking waterfalls. This region experiences one of the 
highest rainfalls in the world, making it rich in forests, unique 
wildlife, and fertile land.
