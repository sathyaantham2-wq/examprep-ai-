Operations with Integers
Can you summarise the rules for integer division looking at the 
above pattern?
In general, for any two positive integers a and b, where b ≠ 0, we can 
say that
a ÷ – b = – (a ÷ b), 
– a ÷ b = – (a ÷ b), and
– a ÷ – b = a ÷ b.
