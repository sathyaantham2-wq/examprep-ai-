0.06 0 Ones + 0 Tenths + 6 Hundredths

When we move from Ones to Tenths we need 
to place the decimal point in the quotient.
0 Tenths ÷ 5 0 Tenths.
6 Hundredths ÷ 5 1 Hundredths, and 
1 Hundredth remains. We need to regroup 1 
Hundredth into 10 Thousandths.
10 Thousandths ÷ 5 2 Thousandths.
So, the quotient is 0.012.
