We observe that the number of black triangles is 1, 3, 9 and 27 
in Stages 0, 1, 2 and 3 respectively. In fact, in every stage, each black 
triangle is replaced with three smaller triangles at the next stage. 
Thus, the number of black triangles at every stage is three times the 
number at the previous stage. 
At Stages 4 and 5 the number of black triangles will be 81 and 243.
