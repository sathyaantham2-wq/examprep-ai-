Example 9 Find the value of cos (–1710°).
Solution We know that values of cos x repeats after an interval of 2π or 360°.
Therefore, cos (–1710°) = cos (–1710° + 5 × 360°)
 = cos (–1710° + 1800°) = cos 90° = 0.
