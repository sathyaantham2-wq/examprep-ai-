In the Jain tradition, the tīrthas are associated with places where 
the Tīrthankaras attained liberation or where significant events 
of their lives occurred. Trees, ponds, hills and mountains that the 
Tīrthankaras visited or meditated at are also considered sacred. 
Examples of such places include Mount Abu, Girnar and the 
Śhatruñjaya hill in Saurashtra (Gujarat).
