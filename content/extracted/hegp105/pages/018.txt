Steps to follow Example for the number 328105
1. Place alternating ‘+’ and 
‘–’ signs before every digit 
starting from the unit’s digit.
–3 + 2 – 8 + 1 – 0 + 5
2. Evaluate the expression. –3 + 2 – 8 + 1 – 0 + 5 = – 3
3. The result denotes the 
remainder obtained when the 
number is divided by 11.
