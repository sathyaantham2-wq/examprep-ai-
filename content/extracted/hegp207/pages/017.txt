Rhombus
Since a rhombus is a parallelogram, the area formula for a parallelogram 
holds for a rhombus as well. However, the additional properties of 
a rhombus give us another method to transform a rhombus into a 
rectangle of the same area by dissection. This method occurs in one of 
the Śulba-Sūtras.
