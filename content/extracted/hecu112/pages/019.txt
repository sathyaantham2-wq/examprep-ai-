Discover, design, and debate
z Plan a clean-up day at school or a nearby park. Wearing 
gloves and using bags, collect the litter you find. Discuss the 
kinds of waste you found. Which was the most common? 
How can we reduce such waste?
z In Arunachal Pradesh, the Nyishi and Mishmi tribes treat the 
Tiger as sacred. In Chhattisgarh, the Baiga tribe worships the 
Bagesur Dev and believes the Tiger is the protector of the 
forest. Find out about other Indian tribes that have a special 
connection with animals.
z Pick a tree near your home or school. Observe it once a week 
for 4 weeks. Note any new leaves, flowers, fruits, or visiting 
birds and insects. Record your observations. You may even 
upload your findings to www.seasonwatch.in and become a 
young citizen scientist.
