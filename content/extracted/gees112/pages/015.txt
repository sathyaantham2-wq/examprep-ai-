261Economic Life Around Us 12 – Understanding Markets
The finished product from the fabric is traded by the 
manufacturing units in the wholesale market. The wholesalers 
are an important channel of supply as they oversee the 
distribution of products to small shopkeepers and big retail 
stores across the country and internationally. They also assess 
how much product is required by retailers. This helps maintain 
the stock of products with the manufacturers and ensures an 
uninterrupted supply of products to the end consumers.
