The cuneiform tablets give us a glimpse into the 
lives and beliefs of the Mesopotamians—their great 
myths and epics, their hymns, the “law codes,” and 
educational treatises. Tablets were also used for 
keeping a record of farming and craft activities, 
especially of potters, seal cutters, shipbuilders, 
carpenters, farm workers, etc.
DON’T MISS OUT
 The Sumerians invented the wheeled cart and sailboat.
 They used calculations for building structures and measuring 
agricultural fields.
 Their number system was based on the number 60, and the 
concepts of the 60-minute hour, 60-second minute, and the 
360-degree circle was invented by the Sumerians.
