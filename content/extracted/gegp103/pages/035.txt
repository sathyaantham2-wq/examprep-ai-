SUMMARY
- We can split a unit into smaller parts to get more exact/accurate
  measurements.
- We extended the Indian place value system and saw that
  * 1 unit = 10 one-tenths,
  * 1 tenth = 10 one-hundredths,
  * 1 hundredth = 10 one-thousandths,
  * 10 one-hundredths = 1 tenth,
  * 100 one-hundredths = 1 unit.
- A decimal point ('.') is used in the Indian place value system to separate
  the whole number part of a number from its fractional part.
- We also learnt how to compare decimal numbers, locate them on the
  number line, and perform addition and subtraction on them.
