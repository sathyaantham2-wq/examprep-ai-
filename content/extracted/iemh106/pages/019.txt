There are two other such formulas for the area of a triangle. Both 
have a connection with circles. See Fig. 6.26.
Given any triangle ABC, there is exactly one circle that passes 
through its three vertices. This is called the circumcircle of ∆ABC. 
There is also exactly one circle that fits tightly in the triangle, 
touching its three sides. This is called the incircle of ∆ABC.
