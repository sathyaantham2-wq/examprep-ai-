9.7 Molecular Mass of Covalent Compounds
Since you know the formulae of the covalent compounds, you can find the 
masses of their molecules by simply adding up the masses of the atoms 
present in them.
Example 9.4 
Molecular mass of water (H2
O)
Atomic mass — H = 1 u; O = 16 u 
Molecular mass of H2
O = (1 u × 2) + (16 u × 1) = 18 u
Example 9.5 
Molecular mass of carbon dioxide (CO2
)
Atomic mass — C = 12 u; O = 16 u
Molecular Mass of CO2
 = (12 u × 1) + (16 u × 2) = 44 u

9.8 Formula Unit Mass of Ionic Compounds
In ionic compounds, the collection of the simplest whole number ratio of 
ions is termed as a formula unit. The mass of a formula unit is called the 
formula unit mass.
Example 9.6 
Formula unit mass of sodium oxide (Na2
O)
Atomic mass — Na = 23 u; O = 16 u 
Formula unit mass of Na2
O = (23 u × 2) + (16 u × 1) = 62 u
