Through the advancements in communication, students can 
access online classes, digital libraries, and educational videos, 
even in remote areas. It aids emergency response during natural 
disasters through quick mobile SMS alerts or apps. Small and 
large businesses sell products across the world through
e-commerce. From quick online payments to e-governance 
where citizens can apply for documents, file complaints, or get 
information easily, progress in communication infrastructure 
has promoted ease of living for communities.
