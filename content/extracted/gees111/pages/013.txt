LET’S EXPLORE 
Organise yourselves into groups of five students each. Take 
up a group project to collect old coins from family members, 
neighbours, shopkeepers, and so on. Document their various 
features—what are they made of, what is the year inscribed 
on the coins, what do you observe on the obverse and reverse 
sides of the coins. What can you guess from your observations? 
How would you know if your guesses are true?
