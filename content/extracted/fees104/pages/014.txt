THINK ABOUT IT
 Both in the earlier picture of a rock shelter and in this one, 
men and women are given certain roles. While they may 
appear to be ‘natural’, they are not necessarily accurate 
and do not cover all situations. For instance, in a rock 
shelter, women may have helped prepare colours for 
painting the rock or may have done some of the painting. 
In both scenes, men may have done some of the cooking or 
may have helped take care of children. 
 Keeping in mind that we have only limited information, 
think about such roles and situations, and discuss in class.
