Over time, human ways of life changed from hunting and 
gathering to agriculture and food production. The ability to produce 
surplus food and material goods laid the foundation for the 
emergence of civilisation.
It is generally agreed that our earliest ancestors evolved and lived 
in Africa and began to move out of the continent around 2 million 
years ago. Homo erectus, an early human ancestor with stone tools, 
such as hand axes and cleavers, was the first hominin to exit Africa. 
These tools have been found in other parts of Asia and Europe, 
indicating their origin in Africa and their dispersion beyond the 
continent between 2 and 0.5 million years ago. Another major wave
