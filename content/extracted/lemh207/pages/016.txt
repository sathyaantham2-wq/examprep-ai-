Example 14 If A and B are two independent events, then the probability of occurrence
of at least one of A and B is given by 1– P(A′) P(B′)
Solution We have
P(at least one of A and B) = P(A ∪ B)
= P(A) + P(B) − P(A ∩ B)
= P(A) + P(B) − P(A) P(B)
= P(A) + P(B) [1−P(A)]
= P(A) + P(B). P(A′)
= 1− P(A′) + P(B) P(A′)
= 1− P(A′) [1− P(B)]
= 1− P(A′) P (B′)
