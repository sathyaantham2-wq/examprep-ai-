Taking Stock
As we stated at the start, this chapter offers only a brief survey 
of the main figures and developments in this turbulent period. 
Numerous dynasties rose and fell, battling one another, but 
sometimes also in alliance with one another. A few of the more 
powerful kingdoms rose to the status of empires, but rarely 
managed to cover a large part of India or to last more than a 
couple of centuries.

 THINK ABOUT IT
We have met some 12 dynasties in this chapter. During the 
same period, many more existed in India: the Bhanjas, Chāpas 
(or Chāvaḍās), Guhilas, Kalachuris, Kadambas, Maitrakas, 
Maukharis, Saindhavas, Śhilāhāras, Somavaṃśhīs, Tomaras, 
Utpalas, Paramāras, Chāhamānas, Gangas, most of them 
with several branches. You need not remember them (except 
that we will meet the last three in the next chapter). The list 
is merely to give a sense of the depth and richness of our 
country’s history.
