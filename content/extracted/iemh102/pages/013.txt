Note that (1, 3) and (7, 15) are also the points on the line y = 2x + 1. Plot 
the points on the line. You may use a graph paper for this task. Observe 
that if a point lies on a line, its coordinates must satisfy the equation 
of the line. Thus, we can verify that (7, 15) lies on the line y = 2x + 1 by 
substituting x = 7 and y = 15 in the equation.
