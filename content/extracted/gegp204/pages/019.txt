This never seems to end! Each time we divide by 3, there 
is a remainder of 1.
Will this process end?
In long division, we see that at each step we get a 
remainder of 1. So, the process will never end!
So, 10 ÷ 3 cannot be expressed using a finite number of 
digits in the decimal form. 
10 ÷ 3 = 3.333 ...
There are decimal divisions where the quotient never ends! We will 
explore such numbers in greater detail in a later class.
