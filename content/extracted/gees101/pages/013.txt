Sand dunes are formed when the wind shifts and shapes the 
sand into hill-like formations. Sometimes these rise as high as 
150 metres. 
The Thar is a vast arid region. Most of it lies within India, 
spanning the states of Rajasthan, Gujarat, Punjab, and Haryana. 
The desert acts as a natural barrier due to the harsh conditions 
that deter human and animal movement — including very high 
daytime temperatures and cold nights, as well as a lack of access 
to water. 
People living in the Thar have adapted their way of life to the 
place and what it offers; food habits, clothing and lifestyle are 
suited to these harsh conditions.
