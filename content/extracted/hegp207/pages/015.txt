How do we find the area of a parallelogram 
by dissecting it into a rectangle?
We have
Area of the parallelogram ABCD = Area of the 
rectangle ABYX. 
Area of the rectangle ABYX = AX × XY.
AX is the height of the parallelogram.

Is there a relation between XY and DC?
Since DX = CY, we get DC = XY by adding the common part XC to DX 
and CY. Since DC is the base of the parallelogram, we get
Area of the parallelogram = base × height.
