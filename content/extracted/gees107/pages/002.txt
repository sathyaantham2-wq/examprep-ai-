146Exploring Society: India and Beyond | Grade 7 Part 1
 Dhruv and Bhavisha had just returned from their journey 
to the Pānḍya kingdom. They had seen the splendid 
markets, taken a short trip on a ship, and met some 
Roman traders buying pearls. Now they were itching to 
use Itihāsa again and jump a few centuries. “What would 
that be like?” they wondered. “Will the cities be the same? 
What about the people and society — would they have 
changed? What kind of governance would they have? 
Would there be new literature, new art?” They could not 
wait to find out and were soon whisked away to …
