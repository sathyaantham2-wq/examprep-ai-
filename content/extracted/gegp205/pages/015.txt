Zero vs. No value
Suppose a player scores 57, 13, 0, 84, — , 51, 27 in a series. Notice that the 
player played Match 3 and scored 0 runs whereas the player did not play 
Match 5. So, we consider the total number of matches to be 6 and not 7. 
We calculate their average runs scored per match as 
(57 + 13 + 0 + 84 + 51 + 27) ÷ 6.
