A Peek Beyond the Point
• The egg of a hummingbird typically is 1.3 cm long and 0.9 cm wide.
• The Philippine Goby is about 0.9 cm long. It can be found in the 
Philippines and other Southeast Asian countries.
• The smallest known jellyfish, Irukandji, has a belly size of 0.5 – 2.5 
cm. Its tentacles can be as long as 1 m. They are found in Australia. 
Its venom can be fatal to humans. 
• The Wolfi octopus, also known as the Star-sucker Pygmy Octopus, 
is the smallest known octopus in the world. Their typical size is 
around 1 – 2.5 cm and they weigh less than 1 gm. They are found 
in the Pacific Ocean.
