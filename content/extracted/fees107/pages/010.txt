The Buddha founded the Sangha, a community of bhikṣhus 
or monks (and, later, bhikṣhuṇīs or nuns) who dedicated 
themselves to practising and spreading his teachings. His 
influence on India, and indeed the whole of Asia, was 
enormous, as we will discover later; it is still perceptible 
today.
“Not by water is one made pure, though 
many people may bathe here [in sacred 
rivers]. But one is pure in whom truth and 
dharma reside.
Conquering oneself is greater than 
conquering a thousand men on the 
battlefield a thousand times.”

disciplined life.

equivalent
