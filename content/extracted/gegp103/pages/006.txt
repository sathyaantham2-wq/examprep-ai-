The length of Shylaja's hand is 12 4/10 units,
and her palm is 6 7/10 units, as shown in the picture. What is the length
of the longest (middle) finger?
The length of the finger can be found by
evaluating (12 + 4/10) - (6 + 7/10). This can be
done in different ways. For example,
(a) 12 + 4/10 - 6 - 7/10
   = (12 - 6) + (4/10 - 7/10)
   = 6 - 3/10
   = 5 + 1 - 3/10
   = 5 + 10/10 - 3/10
   = 5 + 7/10 = 5 7/10
