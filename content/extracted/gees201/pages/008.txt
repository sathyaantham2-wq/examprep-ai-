The rhythm of seasons and crops: 
kharif, rabi, and zaid
Indian agriculture is mainly divided into three cropping seasons: 
kharif, rabi, and zaid. 
Farmers have been following this rhythm for centuries. A wide 
variety of agricultural products becomes available throughout 
the year. This rhythm is one of the factors that ensures food 
supply for the people. The terms kharif, rabi, and zaid are of 
Arabic origin and have been in use since the Mughal times.
