• An algebraic equation is a mathematical statement that indicates the 
equality of two algebraic expressions.
• When the same operation is performed on both sides of an equation, 
equality is maintained.
• Finding a solution to an equation means finding the values of the 
unknowns in the expressions such that the LHS is equal to the RHS. 
• Equations can often be solved by performing the same operation on 
both sides so that the value of the unknown becomes evident.
