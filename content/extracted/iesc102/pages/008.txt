In eukaryotic cells, a network of fine fibres forms the cytoskeleton, which 
provides structural support, maintains cell shape, and enables cell movement 
and internal transport. It is visible only under an electron microscope as 
a separate entity. The cytoplasm may also store starch (in plant cells), or 
crystals of calcium oxalate or silica (in some plant cells). These are known 
as cell inclusions.
