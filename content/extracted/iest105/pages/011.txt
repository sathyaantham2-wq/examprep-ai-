the Satavāhana Empire, which lasted nearly 500 years, from the

India, there were the polities of the Cholas (in the lower Kaveri 
River valley), Pandyas (in the river valleys of the Tamraparni and 
Vaigai), Keralaputras (identified with the Cheras in Kerala), and the 
Satiyaputras (in the northern part of Tamil Nadu). These polities also 
find mention in the Aśhokan inscriptions.
