Example 7.13: For a seesaw having four seats A, B, D, E and 
fulcrum at C (Fig. 7.34), AC = EC = 2 m and BC = DC = 1 m. On 
which seats should children of masses 15 kg and 30 kg sit to 
make the seesaw balanced?
Answer: Suppose the child of mass 15 kg sits on seat A and the 
other child sits at a distance L from the fulcrum. Using Eq. (7.15), 
we obtain
15 kg × 2 m = 30 kg × L
L = 1 m
Thus, the other child should sit at seat D.
