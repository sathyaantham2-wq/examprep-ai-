4.2 Angles in a Quadrilateral
Is it possible to construct a quadrilateral with three angles equal to 90° 
and the fourth angle not equal to 90°?
You might have observed through constructions that this may not be 
possible.
But why not?
This is due to a general property of quadrilaterals related to their 
angles. 
We have seen that the sum of the angles of a triangle is 180°. There is 
a similar regularity in the sum of the angles of a quadrilateral.
