It is through such findings that we know that the Homo habilis (handy 
man) lived in Africa, especially in the Olduvai Gorge in Tanzania and 
Kenya. As noted earlier, their successors Homo erectus, the inventors 
of handaxes and cleavers, were among the first to migrate out of 
Africa and gradually spread into Europe and Asia. While all human 
ancestors were tool makers, it was the Homo sapiens that developed 
complex technologies.
