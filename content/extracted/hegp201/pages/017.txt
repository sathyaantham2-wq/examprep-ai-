Example 5: The rice stock in Raghu’s provision store is getting old. He 
had purchased the rice at ₹35 per kg. To clear his stock, he sells 10 kg rice 
for ₹300. Find out the percentage loss.
The amount Raghu had paid towards 
buying the 10 kg rice is ₹350. He sold it 
for ₹300.
The loss is ₹350 – ₹300 = ₹50.
The percentage loss is 50
350 × 100 = 14.28%.
Could we have just calculated the loss percentage per kg instead? Would
