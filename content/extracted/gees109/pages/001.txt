From the Rulers to 
the Ruled: Types of 
Governments
A ruler’s duties in the internal administration of the country 
are three-fold: rakṣha (protection of the state from external 
aggression), pālana (maintenance of law and order within the 
state), and yogakṣhema (safeguarding the welfare of the people).
Kauṭilya in Arthaśhāstra
(translation by L.N. Rangarajan)
