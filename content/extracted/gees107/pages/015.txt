Fig. 7.14. The yakṣha’s message to the clouds – a scene from Meghadūtam.
Kālidāsa: Little is known about Kālidāsa’s life; legends 
suggest that he was once ridiculed by others, which 
motivated him to work hard and transform his life. He 
is renowned for his contributions to Sanskrit literature 
and exquisitely refined poetry. One of his celebrated 
compositions is Meghadūtam, or ‘The Cloud Messenger’. 
It tells the story of a yakṣha (minor deity), who, after 
being banished from his home by his master, sends a 
message to his beloved through a passing cloud. Apart 
from the many emotions of love, the poem describes in 
great detail the landscapes and weather of north India 
on its journey to the beloved.
