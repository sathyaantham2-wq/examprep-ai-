When one unit is divided into several equal parts, each part is 
called a fractional unit. These are all fractional units: 
1
2 , 1
3 , 1
4 , 1
5 , 1
6 , …, 1
10 , …, 1
50 , …, 1
100 , etc.
We also sometimes refer to fractional units as ‘unit fractions.’
