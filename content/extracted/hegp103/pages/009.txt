III. Number Names Obtained by Counting in Twos
A group of indigenous people in Australia called the Gumulgal had the 
following words for their numbers.
Can you see how their number names are formed?
The number name for 3 is composed of number names of 2 and 1.
The number name for 4 is composed of two occurrences of the number 
name for 2.
Can you see how the names of the other numbers are formed?
The numbers are counted in 2s, using which the number names are 
formed: 3 = 2 + 1, 4 = 2 + 2, 5 = 2 + 2 + 1, 6 = 2 + 2 + 2.
Gumulgal called any number greater than 6 ras.

associated with this number system. Look at the following number
