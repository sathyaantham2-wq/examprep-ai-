Suppose we include a value 11. The new value included is greater 
than the (earlier) median — the median can no longer be 8 as there are 
more values greater than it. Therefore, the median will also increase. 
The median, now, will be the average of the two middle values 8 and 11, 
which is 9.5.
We can similarly argue that when a value less than the median is 
included, the median will decrease.
