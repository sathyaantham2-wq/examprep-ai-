What is the angle between two adjacent lines?
We need the angle between every pair of adjacent lines to be equal. 
Since 360° is equally divided into 8 parts, every angle is 45°.
How do we construct a 45° angle using only a ruler and a compass?
We know how to construct a 90° angle. If we can divide it into two 
equal parts, or bisect it, then we get a 45° angle.
