9. Which is larger? Find out without fully computing the product.
(i) 14 × 26 or 16 × 24
(ii) 25 × 75 or 26 × 74
Ans. 
(i) Let a = 14 × 26 and b = 16 × 24 
Now a = (16 – 2) (24 + 2) 
= 16 × 24 + 16 × 2 – 2 × 24 – 2 × 2 
= b + (32 – 48 – 4) 
= b – 20 
Hence, b > a or 16 × 24 > 14 × 26 
(ii) Let a = 25 × 75 and b = 26 × 74 
Now a = (26 – 1) (74 + 1) = 26 × 74 + 26 – 74 – 1 
= b – 49 
Hence b > a or 26 × 74 > 25 × 75
