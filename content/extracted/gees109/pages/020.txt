4. Oligarchy
The word ‘oligarchy’ is of Greek origin, where olígos means a few 
and árkhō means to rule or command. It is a type of government 
where a small, powerful group makes all the important deci￾sions; usually they are wealthy families or influential people.
In some places in ancient Greece, aristocratic families ruled 
as oligarchies. In modern times, political commentators have 
observed that even some democracies show signs of oligarchy 
when a small group of politicians and wealthy businesspeople 
hold too much influence in governance.
