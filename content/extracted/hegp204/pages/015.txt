Shortest Paths on a Cube
Let us consider an interesting problem related to the discussion so far. 
We know that on a plane, the shortest path between two points is the 
straight line between them. Now, what is the shortest path between two 
points on the surface of a cuboid, if we are allowed to travel only along 
its surface?
Let us imagine a hungry ant living on the surface of a cuboid. To its 
good fortune, there is a laddu on the surface.
