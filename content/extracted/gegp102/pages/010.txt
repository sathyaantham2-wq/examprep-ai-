Example 9: Raghu bought 100 kg of rice from the wholesale market
and packed them into 2 kg packets. He already had four 2 kg packets.
Write an expression for the number of 2 kg packets of rice he has now
and identify the terms.
He had 4 packets. The number of new 2 kg packets of rice is 100 / 2,
which we also write as 100/2.
The number of 2 kg packets he has now is 4 + 100/2. The terms are --
4 and 100/2.

Example 10: Kannan has to pay Rs.432 to a shopkeeper using coins of
Rs.1 and Rs.5, and notes of Rs.10, Rs.20, Rs.50 and Rs.100. How can he
do it?
There is more than one possibility. For example,
432 = 4 x 100 + 1 x 20 + 1 x 10 + 2 x 1
Meaning: 4 notes of Rs.100, 1 note of Rs.20, 1 note of Rs.10 and 2 notes
of Rs.1
432 = 8 x 50 + 1 x 10 + 4 x 5 + 2 x 1
Meaning: 8 notes of Rs.50, 1 note of Rs.10, 4 notes of Rs.5 and 2 notes
of Rs.1

Identify the terms in the two expressions above.
Can you think of some more ways of giving Rs.432 to someone?
