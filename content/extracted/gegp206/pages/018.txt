Construction of a 60° angle
How do we construct a 60° angle?
We get a 60° angle if we construct an equilateral triangle! We can use 
the following steps for this.
Suppose we need a 60° angle at point A on a line segment AX.
Step 1
A B X
Construct an arc with centre A and any radius.
Step 2
C
A B X
With the same radius, cut another arc from B that meets the 
first arc. Let C be the point at which the arcs meet. 
We have ∠CAX = 60°.
