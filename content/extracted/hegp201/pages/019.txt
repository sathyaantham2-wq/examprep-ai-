Taxes
Tax rates, like the GST (Goods and Services Tax) 
rate or the Income Tax rates, are also specified as 
percentages. You may have noticed GST mentioned 
as part of bills. This means that the tax is part of 
the amount we pay and this amount goes to the 
government.

price of a shirt is ₹300, how much will Anwar have to pay to buy
