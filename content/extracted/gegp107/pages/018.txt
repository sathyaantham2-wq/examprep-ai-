We have seen that when two lines are parallel, the internal angles 
on the same side of the transversal add up to 180°. So ∠B = 140°.
So, for what values of ∠B, does a triangle not exist? Does the length 
AB play any part here?
From the discussion above, it can be seen that the length AB does not 
play any part in deciding the existence of a triangle. We can say that a 
triangle does not exist when ∠B is greater than or equal to 140°.
