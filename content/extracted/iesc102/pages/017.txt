At a Glance
y The cell is the basic structural and functional unit of all living 
organisms.
y Prokaryotic cells do not have a well-defined nucleus, instead their 
genetic material is present in a region called the nucleoid. They also 
lack membrane-bound organelles. 
y Eukaryotic cells are larger and more complex. They have a well￾defined nucleus and several membrane-bound organelles. 
y All cells are surrounded by a cell membrane. In addition, cells 
of plants, fungi and bacteria have a cell wall outside the cell 
membrane.
y The nucleus of eukaryotic cells contains chromosomes, which are 
composed of DNA and associated proteins, which carry genetic 
information. 
y All cells are filled with cytoplasm. In eukaryotic cells, cytoplasm 
contains  several  cell  organelles,  each  performing  a  specific  function.
y Important cell organelles include the nucleus, endoplasmic reticulum, 
mitochondria, golgi apparatus, ribosomes and lysosomes. 
y Plant cells also contain special organelles called plastids, such as 
chloroplasts, leucoplasts and chromoplasts.
y Mitosis produces two daughter cells identical to the parent cell. 
y Meiosis is a two-step division process which produces four daughter 
cells, each having half the number of chromosomes. 
y Normal cells grow in a controlled manner, perform their functions 
and die naturally, while cancer cells lose control and keep dividing 
uncontrollably leading to the formation of tumours.
