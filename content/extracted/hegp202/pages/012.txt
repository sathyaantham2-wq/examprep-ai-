• The 4-sided figure obtained (T + U + V) is in fact a square with an 
area equal to the sum of the areas of the two smaller squares!
Why?
• As T, U, X and W are all congruent, the sides of this new 4-sided 
figure all have the same length. Notice the angles.
Explain why all the angles of this new 4-sided figure are right angles and 
so it is a square.
Notice that this new square has as its sidelength, the hypotenuse of the 
right triangle of sides a and b.
• Baudhāyana’s assertion is now clear:
The area of the square on the hypotenuse = the sum of the areas of T, 
U, and V = the sum of the areas of V, W, and X = the sum of the areas of 
the two given squares.
