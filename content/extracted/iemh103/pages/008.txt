Rational numbers are closed under addition, subtraction, and 
multiplication; that is, if one adds two rational numbers, or subtracts 
two rational numbers, or multiplies two rational numbers, a rational 
number is again obtained. Rational numbers are also closed under 
division, provided that one does not divide by zero. That is, the quotient 
of two rational numbers is again a rational number, as long as one does 
not divide by zero.
