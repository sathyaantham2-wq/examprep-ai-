Climate Change
Climate change refers to significant, long-term changes in the 
climate. This may be on the scale of the planet or on a regional 
scale, and it involves shifts in temperature, precipitation and 
weather events. In past millennia (we can go back millions of 
years, in fact), natural processes drove climate change. Since the 
19th century, however, climate change has been largely driven 
by human activities, particularly the burning of fossil fuels, 
deforestation, environmentally harmful industrial practices, and 
production and patterns of excessive or wasteful consumption.
