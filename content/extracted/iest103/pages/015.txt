Climate Change
One of the most urgent challenges that the Earth is facing today is 
climate change. It refers to long-term changes in weather patterns, 
such as temperature, rainfall, and wind, caused mainly by human 
activities, including the burning of fossil fuels, deforestation, and 
industrial pollution that releases gases like carbon dioxide into the 
atmosphere. These actions increase greenhouse gases such as carbon 
dioxide, methane, nitrous oxide, water vapour in the atmosphere, 
trapping heat and raising global temperatures. As a result, we are 
witnessing more frequent floods, droughts, melting of glaciers, 
rising sea levels, and loss of biodiversity. Climate change not only 
threatens ecosystems but also impacts human health, agriculture, 
and livelihoods. You may recall that you have read about ‘climate 
change’ in your grade 8 Science textbook. What actions can be 
taken to reduce such changes? It also has great impact on almost all 
sections of the population including women and children. Tackling 
this issue requires collective efforts—reducing carbon footprints, 
using renewable energy, protecting forests, and adopting sustainable 
lifestyles. Every small step counts, and every human being plays a 
vital role in shaping a healthier and greener future.
