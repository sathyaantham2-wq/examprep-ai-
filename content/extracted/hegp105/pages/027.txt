when divided by 6. He claims, “If you add any three such numbers,

Ans. Let three numbers be 6a + 2, 6b + 2, 6c + 2.

7. When divided by 7, the number 661 leaves a remainder of 3, and 
4779 leaves a remainder of 5. Without calculating, can you say what 
remainders the following expressions will leave when divided by 
7? Show the solution both algebraically and visually.
(i) 4779 + 661 (ii) 4779 – 661
Ans. Algebraically
(i) 4779 = 7p + 5
661 = 7q + 3
4779 + 661 = 7p + 7q + 8
= 7(p + q + 1) + 1 
So remainder is 1
(ii) 4779 − 661 = 7p + 5 − 7q − 3
= 7(p − q) + 2
So remainder is 2.
For visual method refer P. 120 of the textbook.
8. Find a number that leaves a remainder of 2 when divided by 3, 
a remainder of 3 when divided by 4, and a remainder of 4 when 
divided by 5. What is the smallest such number? Can you give a 
simple explanation of why it is the smallest? 
Ans. Given each remainder is 1 less than its divisor.
So the number must be one less from LCM of 3, 4, 5.
LCM of 3, 4, 5 = 3 × 4 × 5 = 60
So smallest number is 60 − 1 = 59, since 60 is the smallest value of 
multiples of 3, 4 and 5.
