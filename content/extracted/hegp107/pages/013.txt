Puneeth’s father went from Lucknow to Kanpur in 2 hours by riding 
his motorcycle at a speed of 50 km/h. If he drives at 75 km/h, how 
long will it take him to reach Kanpur? Can we form this problem as 
a proportion— 
50 : 2 :: 75 : __ 
Would it take Puneeth’s father more time or less time to reach 
Kanpur? Think about it.
Even though this problem looks similar to the previous 
problems, it cannot be solved using the Rule of Three!
The time of travel would actually decrease when the speed 
increases. So this problem cannot be modelled as 50 : 2 :: 75 : __.
