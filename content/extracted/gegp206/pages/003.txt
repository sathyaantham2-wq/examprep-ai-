In ∆AOX and ΔAOY, we already know that AX = AY, and AO is common 
to both triangles.
If we can show that ∠XAO = ∠YAO then, by 
the SAS congruence condition, we can conclude 
that the triangles are congruent.
To show this, we observe that ∆ABX ≅ ΔABY. 
This is so because AX = AY, BX = BY, and AB is 
common to both the triangles.
Thus, we have ∠XAB = ∠YAB, or 
∠XAO = ∠YAO because they are corresponding 
parts of congruent triangles.
Hence, AB is the perpendicular bisector of XY.
