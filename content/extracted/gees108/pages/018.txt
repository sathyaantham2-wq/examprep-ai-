2. List the sacred sites in your region. Enquire into why 
they are considered sacred. Are there stories connected 
with these sacred places? Write a short essay of 150 
words. (Hint: You could speak to elders in your family and 
community, discuss with your teacher, read books and 
articles, etc., to gather relevant information.)

of the people living near them?
