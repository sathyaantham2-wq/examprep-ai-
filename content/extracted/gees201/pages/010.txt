Soil, the Foundation of Cultivation
Soil is the thin, upper layer of the Earth’s crust that supports plant 
life. It is formed over millions of years through the breakdown 
of rocks and the decay of organic matter. It’s the most essential 
resource for farmers, as it provides the necessary nutrients, 
water, and support for crops to grow. This section examines 
the various soils found in the Indian subcontinent and their 
associations with specific crops.
