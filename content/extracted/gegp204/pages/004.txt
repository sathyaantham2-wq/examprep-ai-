Example 3: The distance between Ajay's school and his home is 827 m. 
He walks to school in the morning and then walks back home in the 
evening, 6 days a week. How much does he walk in a week? Answer in 
kilometres.
Each way between school and home, Ajay walks 827 metres, 
i.e., 0.827 km.
So, in a day he walks,
0.827 × 2 = 827
1000 × 2 = 827 × 2
1000 = 
1654
1000 = 1.654
He goes to school 6 days a week. So, in a week, he walks
1.654 × 6 = 1654
1000 × 6 = 1654 × 6
1000 = 
9924
1000 = 9.924
Ajay walks 9.924 km a week.
