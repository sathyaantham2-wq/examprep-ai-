Financial frauds and how to prevent them
Digital payments have made life easier, but users must beware 
of fraud and scams. Fraudsters trick people through fake calls 
or messages to download harmful apps or mislead people into 
sharing bank details or One-Time Passwords (OTPs). This gives 
them access to the user’s mobile or computer, enabling them to 
steal personal data from the device and draining money from 
the bank accounts.
