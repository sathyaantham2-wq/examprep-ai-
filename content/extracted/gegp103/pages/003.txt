3.2 A Tenth Part
The length of the pencil shown in the figure below is 3 4/10 units, which
can also be read as 3 units and four one-tenths, i.e., (3 x 1) + (4 x 1/10)
units.
1/10 + 1/10 + 1/10 + 1/10 + 1/10 + 1/10 + 1/10 + 1/10 + 1/10 + 1/10
= 10 times 1/10 = 10 x 1/10 = 1 unit
This length is the same as 34 one-tenths units because 10 one-tenths
units make one unit. (Three rulers pictured: an eraser measured
between 0 and 3 units, a pencil measured at 3 4/10 units, a sharpener
measured between 0 and 2 units.)
