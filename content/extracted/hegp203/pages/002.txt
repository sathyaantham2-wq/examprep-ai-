3.2 Ratios in Maps
Have you noticed that in many maps there is a ratio given, usually in 
the lower right corner of the map? It usually contains 1 and a very large 
number, such as 1 : 60,00,000. What does RF 1 : 60,00,000 mean? What 
does it indicate? Can you guess?
A Representative Fraction (RF) is an expression that shows the ratio 
between a distance on the map and the corresponding actual distance 
on the ground. 
For example, if the ratio on a map is 1 : 60,00,000, that means a distance 
of 1 cm on the map is equivalent to a geographical distance of 60,00,000 cm. 
Remember, this is geographical distance and not road distance!
Convert 60,00,000 cm to kilometres.
It is 60 km. Verify this.
