Build it in Your Imagination
We will start this section by practising visualisation. For each prompt, 
feel free to talk to your partner, gesture, draw it in the air — but do not 
actually draw on paper!
My method is different. I do not rush into 
actual work. When I get an idea I start at once 
building it up in my imagination. I change the 
construction, make improvements, and operate 
the device entirely in my mind.
— Nikolas Tesla (1856 – 1943), the great 
Serbian-American engineer and inventor, who 
made fundamental contributions to electrical 
engineering and other fields.
