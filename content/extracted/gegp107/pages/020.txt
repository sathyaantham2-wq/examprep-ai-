Therefore, ∠XAB = 50°, and ∠YAC = 70°. Can we find ∠BAC from this?
We know that ∠XAB, ∠YAC and ∠BAC together form 180°. So
∠XAB + ∠YAC + ∠BAC = 180°
50° + ∠BAC + 70° = 180°
120° + ∠BAC = 180°
Thus, ∠BAC = 60°
