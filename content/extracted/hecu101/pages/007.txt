to the most complicated are done. This is the idea of systematic 
investigation. And just so you know, even this simple everyday 
observation—of a puri puffing—is not really completely 
understood by scientists today! 
So, whether it is the puffing of a puri or the shrinking bright 
part of the Moon after purnima, let your careful observations 
guide you along your explorations into the investigate world 
of science.
